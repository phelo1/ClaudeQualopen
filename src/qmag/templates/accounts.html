Adding an account

Every account gets its own desk: a state directory with its own journal, settings (broker credentials, risk budget) and daemon. That keeps sizing, the kill switch, the daily loss limit and the trade journal per account, and means a fill on one account is never assumed on another.

  1. On the server: bash deploy/add-desk.sh NAME --broker alpaca --port 8856 (brokers: paper, alpaca, ibkr, mt5; a second IBKR account needs its own gateway on other ports). This creates desks/NAME, its qmag-daemon-NAME / qmag-dashboard-NAME services and adds it to this desk's {{ desks_env }}.
  2. Put that account's keys in the new desk's settings page (its own dashboard port) or its settings.env.
  3. Anywhere else: run qmag daemon --broker … --state-dir /path and list NAME=/path in Other desks to show on the settings page.

A desk you only want to watch, not trade from: create it, then throw its kill switch — its holdings and P&L still show here.
