Guard rails

These are observational comparisons, not causal effects. Shadow outcomes use a simplified fill model and cannot authorize changes. Applied tightenings require fresh verified evidence; the same historical trades cannot be reused after a change. At most one knob moves per review, inside its declared bounds and outside its cooldown.

Autonomous promotion is configured separately in settings → Autonomy. Legacy review auto-application is ignored while the autonomous controller is enabled.
